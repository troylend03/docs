## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My broker isn't available for connection">
    We're continually adding new brokerages. Check our [supported brokerages list](/portfolio/supported-brokerages) to see what's currently available. If yours isn't listed, you can [add your holdings manually](/portfolio/add-manually) or [contact us](/support/contact) to request your broker be added.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I want a refund">
    You can request a refund through your account settings or by contacting our support team. See our [refund request guide](/billing/request-refund) and [refund policy](/billing/refund-policy) for details on eligibility and timing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I can't log in to my account">
    Try [resetting your password](/account/reset-password) first. If that doesn't work, check our [login troubleshooting guide](/troubleshooting/cant-log-in) for solutions to common login issues including email verification, social login, and browser problems.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I cancel my subscription?">
    You can cancel anytime from your account settings. Your access continues through the end of your billing period. See [how to cancel](/billing/cancel) for step-by-step instructions and [what happens after cancellation](/billing/what-happens-after-cancel).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I connect my portfolio?">
    Go to the Portfolio section in Rallies and follow the connection flow to securely link your brokerage account. See our [portfolio connection guide](/portfolio/connect-brokerage) for detailed instructions and [troubleshooting tips](/portfolio/connection-issues) if you run into issues.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
