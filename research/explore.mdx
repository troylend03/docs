# Explore & Discover

The Explore section lets you browse investment portfolios from hedge funds, politicians, and curated investment themes. See what professional investors are holding and discover stocks grouped by compelling themes.

## What You'll Find

### Hedge Fund Portfolios

Track the holdings of major hedge funds based on their 13F filings:

- **Berkshire Hathaway** — Warren Buffett's legendary value investing portfolio
- **Pershing Square** — Bill Ackman's concentrated activist positions
- **Scion Asset** — Michael Burry's contrarian bets
- **Appaloosa LP** — David Tepper's macro-driven portfolio
- **Tiger Global** — Tech-focused growth investing
- **Bridgewater Associates** — Ray Dalio's all-weather approach
- **Soros Fund** — George Soros's global macro positions
- **Duquesne Family** — Stanley Druckenmiller's portfolio
- **Elliott Investment** — Paul Singer's activist positions

Each fund shows:
- Number of holdings
- YTD return performance
- Portfolio composition

### Politician Portfolios

See what members of Congress are buying and selling based on their required financial disclosures:

- **Nancy Pelosi** — Former House Speaker's trades
- **Marjorie Taylor Greene** — Representative's portfolio
- **Ro Khanna** — Silicon Valley representative's holdings
- **Tommy Tuberville** — Senator's investment activity
- **Josh Gottheimer** — Representative's trades
- And many more...

Each politician profile shows:
- Number of holdings

> ⚠️ **Note:** Politician trades are disclosed after the fact (up to 45 days later). This data is for research purposes only.

## How to Use Explore

### On Web

1. Click **"Explore"** in the left sidebar
2. Browse categories in the left panel:
   - **Funds** — Hedge fund portfolios
   - **Politicians** — Congressional trading
   - **Themes** — Curated stock collections
3. Click any card to view full holdings
4. Use the search bar to find specific portfolios

### On Mobile

1. Tap **"Explore"** in the navigation
2. Swipe between Funds, Politicians, and Themes
3. Tap any card to view details

## Understanding the Data

### Performance Metrics

Each portfolio card shows:
- **Holdings** — Number of stocks in the portfolio
- **YTD Return** — Year-to-date performance (green = positive, red = negative)

### Data Sources

- **Hedge funds:** 13F filings with the SEC (updated quarterly)
- **Politicians:** Congressional financial disclosures (updated as filed)
- **Themes:** Curated by Rallies based on investment thesis

### Limitations

- 13F data is delayed (filed 45 days after quarter end)
- Politician disclosures can be delayed up to 45 days
- Holdings represent a snapshot in time
- Performance calculations are approximate
