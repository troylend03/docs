# Understanding Stock Pages

Stock pages are your command center for researching any company. Every stock in Rallies has a dedicated page with price data, financials, charts, analyst info, and more. This guide walks you through everything you'll find there.

## Getting to a Stock Page

### Search

1. Use the **search bar** at the top of the screen
2. Type a ticker symbol (AAPL) or company name (Apple)
3. Click the stock from the results
4. You're on the stock page

### From Watchlist

Click any stock in your watchlist to go directly to its page.

### From Portfolio

Click any holding in your portfolio to view its stock page.

### From AI Chat

When the AI mentions a stock, click the ticker to open its page.

## Stock Page Overview

When you land on a stock page, here's what you'll see:

### Header Section

At the very top:
- **Ticker symbol** — AAPL
- **Current price** — Real-time for Pro, 15-min delayed for Free
- **Today's change** — Percentage change with color indicator
- **Share button** — Share the stock page

### Navigation Tabs

Stock pages have five main tabs:

| Tab | What It Shows |
|-----|---------------|
| **Digest** | AI-generated news summary and insights |
| **Fundamentals** | Financial statements and metrics |
| **Funds** | Which hedge funds hold this stock |
| **Politicians** | Congressional trading activity |
| **Analysts** | Wall Street ratings and price targets |

### Price Chart

The interactive chart shows price history:

**Timeframes:** Today, Week, YTD, Year, All
**Chart types:** Line, Candle
**Features:**
- **Indicators** — Add technical overlays
- **Targets** — Show analyst price targets on chart
- **Chart AI** — Get AI analysis of the chart pattern

Hover over the chart to see exact prices at any point in time.

### Key Statistics

Below the chart, a row of important metrics:

| Metric | What It Tells You |
|--------|-------------------|
| **Market Cap** | Total company value (price × shares) |
| **P/E Ratio** | Price relative to earnings |
| **EPS** | Earnings per share |
| **Analyst Target** | Average Wall Street price target |
| **Revenue** | Total annual revenue |
| **Dividend Yield** | Annual dividend as % of price |

## Stock Page Tabs

### Digest Tab

The Digest tab provides AI-powered insights about the stock:

**What's the latest?**
AI-generated summary of recent news and events affecting the stock, including:
- Breaking news analysis
- Market context
- Key developments

**Go deeper**
Suggested questions to explore, like:
- "What's AAPL's earnings quality?"
- "How does AAPL compare to its competitors?"
- "Is AAPL a good long-term investment?"

**Upcoming Events**
Calendar of important dates like earnings reports and economic data releases.

### Fundamentals Tab

The Fundamentals tab shows financial data with an interactive chart:

**Revenue & Net Income Chart**
Visual comparison of revenue and net income over time (quarterly or yearly view).

**Metrics Table**
Toggle between Quarter and Yearly views to see:

| Category | Metrics Included |
|----------|-----------------|
| **Profitability** | Revenue, Gross Profit, Net Income, Profit Margin, EPS |
| **Cash** | Cash, Operating Cash Flow |
| **Balance Sheet** | Total Assets, Total Liabilities, Equity, Current Assets |
| **Valuation** | PE Ratio |
| **Growth** | Research And Development, Operating Income |

**Expandable Sections**
Click to expand detailed views:
- **Income Statement** — Full P&L breakdown
- **Balance Sheet** — Assets and liabilities detail
- **Cash Flows** — Operating, investing, financing activities
- **Comprehensive Income** — Other income items

### Funds Tab

See which hedge funds and institutional investors own this stock:

**What You'll See**
- List of funds holding the stock
- Position size and allocation
- Recent changes (increased, decreased, new position)

This data comes from 13F filings with the SEC, updated quarterly.

### Politicians Tab

Track congressional trading activity in this stock:

**What You'll See**
- Which politicians have traded this stock
- Buy/sell activity
- Transaction dates and amounts

> ⚠️ **Note:** Politicians must disclose trades within 45 days, so this data may be delayed.

### Analysts Tab

**Consensus Rating**
Visual radar chart showing the distribution of analyst opinions across:
- Strong Buy
- Buy
- Hold
- Sell
- Strong Sell

**Average Target**
The consensus price target from Wall Street analysts.

**Rating History Table**
Detailed log of recent analyst activity:

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Date** | When the rating was issued |
| **Firm** | Investment bank or research firm |
| **Analyst** | Individual analyst name (when available) |
| **Action** | Maintained, Raised, Lowered, or Initiated |
| **Target** | New price target |
| **Change** | Dollar change from previous target |

## Using the AI on Stock Pages

Every stock page has AI integration:

### Quick Ask

Click **"Ask AI"** to start a conversation about this stock. The AI already knows which stock you're looking at.

**Try:**
> "What's the bull case?"
> "How does the valuation look?"
> "Any concerns I should know about?"

### Deep Research

Click **"Deep Research"** (Pro) to generate a comprehensive analysis report on the spot.

### Contextual Questions

In chat, you can reference "this stock" and the AI understands:
> "Compare this stock to its main competitor"

## Comparing Stocks

Want to compare multiple companies? 

1. Open a stock page
2. Click **"Compare"** 
3. Add other stocks to compare
4. View metrics side-by-side

Or ask the AI:
> "Compare AAPL, MSFT, and GOOGL"

## Stock Page Actions

### Add to Watchlist

Click the **"+ Watchlist"** button to add the stock to any of your watchlists.

### Set Price Alert

Click the **bell icon** to set a price alert for this stock.

### Share

Copy the link to share the stock page with someone.

## Data Freshness

Understanding when data updates:

| Data Type | Update Frequency |
|-----------|------------------|
| Price | Real-time (Pro) / 15-min delay (Free) |
| Volume | Real-time |
| Financials | Quarterly (after earnings) |
| Analyst ratings | As published |
| Insider trades | As filed (up to 2 days after trade) |
| News | Continuous |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is some data missing for certain stocks?

Data availability varies by company:
- Small caps may have less analyst coverage
- International stocks may have limited data
- Very new IPOs take time to build data history

### What's the difference between the stock page and asking the AI?

The stock page shows structured data at a glance. The AI can explain, analyze, and contextualize that data. Use both—the page for quick reference, the AI for deeper understanding.

### Can I customize what I see on stock pages?

Some display preferences may be adjustable in settings. The core data sections are standard for all users.

### How do I know if data is real-time or delayed?

Pro users see real-time data. Free users see 15-minute delayed data. Check the timestamp near the price for confirmation.
